🛶 Rowing Boats Quiz 🐶

Let's test your reading skills! Have fun!

Part 1: Multiple Choice (4 Questions)

1. Who said, "We will take those rowing boats"?

Correct Answer: B. Dad

2. Where did the family stop for the picnic?

Correct Answer: C. On a grassy bank (but not by the stones)

3. What was the loud noise Wilf heard while eating?

Correct Answer: B. Voices of Roy and Joe

4. How did Joe fall into the river?

Correct Answer: B. He was trying to throw a rope

Correct Answer: floating

7. Floppy got the rope in his ______.

Example Answer: Because the boat was floating away and he couldn't catch it.
