🌪️ In a Spin 🌪️

Help Max and Ant with their windy adventure!

Part 1: Story Time (Multiple Choice)

1. What was the weather like in the park?
Correct Answer: C) It was a windy day.
2. What did Ant pick up from the ground?
Correct Answer: A) A funny shaped wing/seed.
3. What did the bird use to pick up Ant?
Correct Answer: B) Its beak.
4. Where did Ant land at the end of the story?
Correct Answer: C) On the ground.

Part 3: Mini Detective (Short Answer)

8. What happened when Ant waved his arms and kicked his legs?
Example Answer: The bird let Ant go / Ant started to fall.
9. Why did Max call Ant a "flying ant"?
Example Answer: Because Ant spun down slowly with the seed / He looked like he was flying.
