The Strange Beast Quiz

Can you solve the mystery of the strange beast? 🍎

Part 1: Choose the Best Answer 🧐

1. At the beginning of the story, what animal was Wilma thinking of?

Correct Answer: B. An elephant (She said it had 4 legs and later a long nose!)

2. Where did the magic key take the children?

Correct Answer: B. To a castle in the past

3. What were the people in the crowd waiting to see?

Correct Answer: A. A strange beast

4. How did the elephant feel when it heard all the noise?

Correct Answer: C. Frightened (Wilma said it was not angry, but frightened.)
