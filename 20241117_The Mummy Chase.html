The Mummy Chase

Can you escape the pyramid? Let's test your memory!

Part 1: Story Check ✅

1. Why did Seb and Neema go into the pyramid?

A. To find gold.
B. To get cool because it was too hot.
C. To find a lost cat.
Correct Answer: B. To get cool because it was too hot.

2. Who told Neema "Do not press that!"?

A. Seb
B. Mum
C. The guide
Correct Answer: C. The guide

3. What did the mummy do after Neema pressed the stone?

A. It started to moan and chase them.
B. It gave them a gift.
C. It ran away from the children.
Correct Answer: A. It started to moan and chase them.

4. How did the children stop the mummy?

A. They hit it with a stick.
B. They tickled it.
C. They gave it a fan.
Correct Answer: B. They tickled it.

Part 2: Word Fun 📝

Word Bank: chase, stone, mummy, ticklish, steps

5. Neema looked at a in the pyramid.

Correct Answer: stone

6. They ran up the and up.

Correct Answer: steps

7. Neema said, "Look! It is !"

Correct Answer: ticklish

Part 3: Think and Write 💡

8. What sound did the mummy make?

Example Answer: It started to moan. (Hee-hee-hee when tickled)

9. Where did Seb want to put the mummy back?

Example Answer: He wanted to get the mummy back on its box.

Part 4: Read Aloud 🗣️

10. Please read this sentence aloud:

"It's good to see Mum, but not a mummy!"

(Ask your teacher or parent to check your pronunciation!)

Great job reading! This was a joke about two types of 'mummies'.
