Part 1: Multiple Choice (選擇題)

1. What did Phil use to talk to the people on the beach?

A. A telephone
B. A loud hailer
C. A whistle
Correct Answer: B. A loud hailer

2. How many extra pegs did the family put in the tent?

A. Six
B. Ten
C. Eight
Correct Answer: C. Eight

3. Who was afraid and began to howl during the storm?

A. Floppy
B. Chip
C. Biff
Correct Answer: A. Floppy

4. What game did the children play inside the tent?

A. Cards
B. Snakes and ladders
C. Tag
Correct Answer: B. Snakes and ladders
