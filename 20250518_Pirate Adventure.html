🏴‍☠️ Pirate Adventure! 🦜

Help Biff, Chip, Wilf, and Wilma on their magic journey!

Part 1: Choose the Best Answer 🔍

1. At the beginning, what does Biff think about pirates?

Correct Answer: She doesn't like them.

2. What happens to the children when the magic key glows?

Correct Answer: They get smaller and smaller.

3. Where do the children find themselves after going through the magic door?

Correct Answer: On a sandy beach with the sea.

4. Why did the pirates take the children to their ship?

Correct Answer: To have a party because nobody else came.
