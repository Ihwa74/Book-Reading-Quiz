Welcome, young Viking! Let's test your adventure knowledge!

Part 1: Multiple Choice (选择题)

1. What does Erik's dad do?

Correct Answer: B. He is a Viking trader.

2. What items are they loading onto the ship at the harbour?

Correct Answer: B. Fur and leather.

3. Where do Erik and the Vikings sleep on the ship?

Correct Answer: C. On the deck.

4. What did Erik use to buy presents for his mum and sister?

Correct Answer: B. Silver coins.
