Level 5 Story Adventure

Part 1: Story Check (選擇題)
1. Where was the box?
Correct Answer: By Chip's bed
2. What was inside the box?
Correct Answer: A magic key
3. What happened when the magic began?
Correct Answer: Biff and Chip got smaller
4. What did the little mouse do?
Correct Answer: It ran away
Part 2: Vocabulary (選填題)
Word Bank: [ bigger / glowing / magic / slippers / pin ]
5. Something was _______ inside the box.
Correct Answer: glowing
6. Biff picked up a _______.
Correct Answer: pin [cite: 58]
7. At the end, they got ______ and ______ and ______.
Correct Answer: bigger
