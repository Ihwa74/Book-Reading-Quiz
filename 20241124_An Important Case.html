🌲 An Important Case 🌲

Let's test your reading skills!

Part 1: Multiple Choice (選擇題) 📚

1. What were the children playing outside at the beginning?

A. Basketball
B. Football
C. Tag
Correct Answer: B. Football

2. Who kicked the ball into the bush?

A. Kipper
B. Wilma
C. Wilf
Correct Answer: C. Wilf

3. What did Kipper find in the bush?

A. A red case
B. A lost puppy
C. A new football
Correct Answer: A. A red case

4. Could Dad open the case?

A. Yes, he did.
B. No, he couldn't.
Correct Answer: B. No, he couldn't.

Part 2: Word Bank (選填題) ✍️

Word Bank: police | outside | stolen | sorry | important

5. The children were playing __________.

Correct Answer: outside

6. The case looks very, very __________.

Correct Answer: important

7. Two __________ officers came to look at the case.

Correct Answer: police

Part 3: Short Answer (簡答題) 💬

8. How did the man come to the house?

Example Answer: He came in a big car.

9. What was really inside the case at the end?

Example Answer: There were sandwiches (and an apple).

Part 4: Read Aloud (朗讀題) 🎤

10. Please read this sentence aloud:

"It's my case," he said. "Thank you. It was stolen."

(Self-check: Did you read it clearly?)
