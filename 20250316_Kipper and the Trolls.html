Part 1: Multiple Choice (選擇題)

1. What did Mum want to make in the kitchen?

Correct Answer: B. A cake

2. Why did Kipper run out of the room?

Correct Answer: C. He didn't want Mum to cut his nails

3. Where did the magic take Biff and Kipper?

Correct Answer: A. To a stream with a bridge

4. What was the color of the key when it glowed?

Correct Answer: C. Golden/Yellow (Glow)
