🔊 The Noisy Day

Can you help Cat find some quiet? 🤫

Part 1: Choose the Best Answer 📝

1. What time did Cat's clock go BEEP BEEP BEEP?

Correct Answer: 07:30AM

2. How many sisters does Cat have?

Correct Answer: Three

3. Where did Cat go to hide during "break" time at school?

Correct Answer: In the hall

4. What were the boys doing in the playground?

Correct Answer: Playing football

Part 2: Fill in the Blanks 🔠

[ quiet ] [ tiny ] [ shake ] [ loud ] [ ears ]

5. Suddenly the band came in and soon the floor began to ______.

Correct Answer: shake

6. Being ______ was a problem, loud noise hurt her little ______!

Correct Answer: tiny, ears

7. At last the house was very ______.

Correct Answer: quiet

Part 3: Short Answer ✍️

8. What did Cat do at dinner time to get away from the noise?

Example Answer: She went off to have a read / She read a book.

9. What happened right after the house became quiet at the end?

Example Answer: Cat began to snore! / She made a ZZZZZZ noise.
