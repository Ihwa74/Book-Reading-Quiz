Part 1: Choose the Best Answer (Multiple Choice)

1. What was Ho's job?

A. He was a rich farmer.
B. He looked after cattle.
C. He made bread.
Correct Answer: B. He looked after cattle.

2. Who gave Ho the golden paintbrush?

A. A rich farmer.
B. A young child.
C. A thin old man.
Correct Answer: C. A thin old man.

3. What happened when Ho painted the hay?

A. It became real.
B. It stayed on the paper.
C. It blew away in the wind.
Correct Answer: A. It became real.

4. Why did Ho paint a blue stream?

A. To wash his paintbrush.
B. Because the stream was dry and animals were thirsty.
C. To help the farmer swim.
Correct Answer: B. Because the stream was dry and animals were thirsty.
