🛷 Husky Adventure 🐾

Let's test your adventure memory! ❄️

Part 1: Multiple Choice 🔘

1. Why did Floppy run to Biff's bedroom to hide?

A. He was cold.
B. He didn't want to pull the sled.
C. He wanted to sleep.
Correct Answer: B. He didn't want to pull the sled.

2. What did the wolves in the forest look like?

A. Yellow eyes and small teeth.
B. Red eyes and long white teeth.
C. Green eyes and no teeth.
Correct Answer: B. Red eyes and long white teeth.

3. What happened after Floppy hit a tree?

A. He barked loudly.
B. He ran away from a man.
C. He lay in the snow with his eyes shut.
Correct Answer: C. He lay in the snow with his eyes shut.

4. Where did the man need to take his sick boy?

A. To the forest.
B. To the hospital.
C. To Biff's house.
Correct Answer: B. To the hospital.

Part 2: Word Bank 📝

Word Bank: hospital | faster | husky | cross | magic

5. The dogs at the sled were not wolves, they were _________ dogs.

Correct Answer: husky

6. Floppy was _________ when the big dog called him a "floppy dog."

Correct Answer: cross

7. The sled went _________ and faster until the other dogs panted.

Correct Answer: faster
